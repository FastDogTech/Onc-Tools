Privacy

The values you enter never leave your device.

  • No data is sent anywhere. All calculations run locally in your browser.
  • No accounts or sign-in. There is nothing to register for.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising. No third-party scripts, fonts or trackers are loaded.
  • Saved data stays on your device. Settings and History are kept in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded or synced. Clearing your browser's site data, or tapping Clear in History, removes it.
  • Works offline. After the first load the app is cached on your device.
