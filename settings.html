About Onc Tools

Onc Tools is intended as a reference only for healthcare professionals with common oncology calculations and date management tasks.

Disclaimer: This app is intended for healthcare professionals and should be used as a reference tool only. Always verify calculations and consult appropriate medical guidelines. Always refer to your facility's policies and guidelines.

All values you enter stay on this device in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent to a server.
